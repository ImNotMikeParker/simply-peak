# Elgato Marketplace listing copy

**Name:** Simply Peak

**Summary (short):** Control your Puffco Peak Pro from Stream Deck: profiles, hold-to-start sessions, boost, battery,
lights, and a full-width session bar on Stream Deck +.

**Description:**

Simply Peak puts a Puffco Peak Pro on your Stream Deck over Bluetooth.

- Four Profile keys with each profile's name, temperature, time and colour. The active one is outlined.
- Start / Stop with hold-to-start, live chamber temperature, a countdown, and a flash the moment it is ready.
- Boost, Battery (with sessions remaining), Session Stats, Lantern, Stealth, Sleep / Power Off, and a Connection key.
- Stream Deck +: a Session dial (rotate for temperature, time or profile; hold to start; tap to boost), a Session
  Strip you place on all four dials for one bar across the whole strip that fills while heating and drains through
  the session, Brightness, and Battery.
- Fahrenheit or Celsius.

Works with Peak Pro on firmware X or later, on Windows. The Peak connects to one controller at a time, so close the
Puffco app on your phone while using the deck. Everything the plugin does goes through the device's own safety limits;
it cannot exceed what the Puffco app allows.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Puffco. Intended for adults 21+. Open source (MIT).
