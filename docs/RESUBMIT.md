## The new images, against the gallery rules

Rules from https://docs.elgato.com/guidelines/products#gallery:

- **At least 3 gallery items, PNG, 1920 x 960:** there are 4 gallery images plus the thumbnail, and every one is exactly 1920 x 960.
- **Accurately depicts the product and what it does:** every key, touch-strip panel and settings panel is drawn by the plugin's own code. Keys come from `src/render/svg.ts`, the strip panels from the plugin's `layouts/*.json`, filled with the same values `src/actions/dials.ts` sends, and the settings panel is the plugin's real `ui/session.html`. All the numbers agree across the images (Strong Vapor is 531°F everywhere).
- **Elgato devices accurate and true to the product:** the Stream Deck + is drawn with its real layout (4 x 2 keys, the 800 x 100 touch strip, 4 dials), and the Stream Deck MK.2 with its 5 x 3 keys. No Elgato or Puffco photos or logos are used.
- **Text clear and legible, kept to a minimum:** each image has one headline and at most one short line. Detail stays in the description.
- **English, no imagery we do not own the rights to, no copying other Makers:** all met. Everything is generated by the project's scripts.
- **Recommendations:** gallery-2 is a close-up of a feature (the session cycle). Gallery-3 is the touch strip, which only this plugin does. Gallery-4 shows the settings panel and the per-dial layouts.

## Demo video shot list (about 2 to 3 minutes)

Before you record, empty and clean the chamber. Elgato needs to see the controls work, not the device being used, so run
every heat cycle empty and keep the video suitable for a general audience. Disconnect the Puffco phone app, because the
Peak only talks to one controller at a time.

Framing: one camera on a tripod (a phone is fine) with the Stream Deck and the Peak Pro both in frame, so every press and
the device's response show up in the same shot. Record at 1080p. A screen recording of the Stream Deck app is a nice
extra for shot 1, but it is not required.

## Resubmission steps

From https://docs.elgato.com/maker-console/review-process#submitting-revisions: you can fix a rejected product either
by submitting a new version or by submitting a revision of the rejected version. Use a revision here.

1. Record the video and send the email above (Elgato wants the video before they approve).
2. In Maker Console, open the Simply Peak product and replace its media: upload `assets/marketplace/thumbnail.png` as
   the thumbnail, and `gallery-1.png` to `gallery-4.png` as the gallery, removing the old gallery images. Save.
3. Go to **Products**, select **Simply Peak**, then open the **Versions** tab.
4. Select the rejected **0.2** version. The "Revise version" dialog opens.
5. Click **Select a file** and choose `C:\Users\mjp58\source\repos\puffco-streamdeck\com.mjp.simplypuffco.streamDeckPlugin`
   (its version is 0.2.0.0, which matches).
6. Leave the release notes as they are. Turn on "Automatically publish after being approved" if you want it to go live
   by itself.
7. Click **Submit for review**. The status changes to Pending review. Reviews take 4 to 10 working days, and Elgato
   replies by email from maker@elgato.com.
